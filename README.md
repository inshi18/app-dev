## ⭐ Movie Description

*Spider-Man: No Way Home* is a superhero movie about Peter Parker, whose identity as Spider-Man becomes public. He asks Doctor Strange for help, but a spell goes wrong and brings visitors from other universes into his world.

The movie shows the importance of **friendship, responsibility, courage, and sacrifice**.

## 👥 Main Characters

- MJ
- Ned Leeds
- Green Goblin
- Doctor Octopus
